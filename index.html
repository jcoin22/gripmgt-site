Why Lakewood Ranch Homeowners Love Soft Washing

Soft washing isn’t just a substitute for pressure washing—it’s a smarter, longer-lasting way to restore and protect your home’s exterior. Here’s why so many Florida homeowners choose this gentle yet powerful method:

1. Deep Clean That Lasts

Algae, mold, and mildew aren’t simple stains—they’re living growths that eat away at your roof, siding, and paint. Our professional soft wash system eliminates these contaminants at the root using eco-friendly cleaning blends that kill growth and prevent its return.

2. Uses Less Water, Creates a Bigger Impact

Instead of relying on high pressure and gallons of water, soft washing leverages chemistry and dwell time. This method can use up to 70% less water than traditional pressure washing, delivering a cleaner finish and a smaller environmental footprint.

3. Safe for Families, Pets, and Landscaping

Every cleaning product we use is low-toxicity, biodegradable, and safe for people, pets, and plants. We take extra care to protect your landscaping and always rinse down all surfaces after every job.

4. Instantly Boosts Curb Appeal

Nothing transforms a property faster than a clean exterior. Whether you’re preparing to sell or just want to freshen your look, a soft wash makes your home brighter, newer, and more welcoming.

5. Prevents Damage and Costly Repairs

Dirt, algae, and grime can lead to rot, peeling paint, or roof damage if left untreated. Soft washing is more than a cosmetic upgrade—it’s preventative maintenance that extends the life of your materials.

Roof Washing Done the Right Way

Never allow anyone to pressure wash your roof. High-pressure systems can strip protective granules, loosen shingles, and even void your warranty. Our process follows ARMA (Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association) guidelines—using a low-pressure chemical rinse that thoroughly removes growth without harming your roof.

“High-pressure washing systems are likely to damage asphalt roofing and should not be used on asphalt roofing for removing algae or for any other purpose.” — ARMA
